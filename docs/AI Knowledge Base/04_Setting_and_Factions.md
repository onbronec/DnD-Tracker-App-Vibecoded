# Setting and Factions — Mechanical Reference

This file contains only setting information that is useful for monster and encounter design.

It is not intended to be a complete campaign encyclopedia.

---

## Epic Campaign Tone

The campaign operates far above normal 5e power assumptions.

Important enemies may:

- exceed normal ability-score limits;
- ignore or break standard 5e expectations;
- use custom resources;
- take multiple turns;
- survive 0 HP;
- reshape battlefields;
- interact directly with Epic spells and effects.

Despite the scale, mechanics should remain readable and offer meaningful player decisions.

---

## Imateria (Far Realm) and the Seven Ancient Principles

**Imateria**, also called the **Far Realm**, is a source of alien power and corruption opposed by the power of Creation. **Daemoni** come from Imateria, serve the **Seven Ancient Principles**, and are the precursors of demons and devils. Each Principle has its own demonic servants. Imateria's influence is **not equivalent to moral evil**: corruption/Taint describes metaphysical contact or alteration, not whether a being's intentions are good or bad.

The Seven are governing **principles** whose forces shape the world. Each is sufficiently powerful and influential that mortals could plausibly mistake it for **the** governing force of existence. Their domains, personalities, and follower names are distinct; these names are not interchangeable faction tags.

### 1. Ambice — Asgorath

**Asgorath, the dragon goddess**, currently leads the Seven Principles. She is the most *human* in disposition and embodies the archetypal dragon. Her followers are called **Moc**.

### 2. Agonie

A female entity that loves **pain and suffering**, seeing them as paths to growth and self-realization. **Strife is the purpose of existence** to her. Her followers are called **Očista**.

### 3. Pandemonium (Chaos)

A male Principle inspired by **Khorne**: chaotic, escalating violence and slaughter. His followers are called **Svoboda**. The separate Pandemonium section below describes the established combat identity of his servants.

### 4. Fanatismus

A female Principle centered on the **negative aspects of faith** and fanatic devotion. Her followers are called **Oddanost**.

### 5. Změna

A Principle associated with **both genders**, inspired by **Tzeentch**. Followers of Změna are called **Růst**.

### 6. Hedonismus

A female Principle inspired by **Slaanesh**, associated with hedonism. Her followers are called **Potěšení**.

### 7. Entropie

A male, profoundly **otherworldly** Principle: **the most powerful of the Seven**, yet **apathetic**. He is the **father of plague**. Unlike the others, his motivation was not the pursuit of power, but the **annihilation of the other Lightbringers**. His followers are called **Odpočinek**.

### Cosmological Notes for Monster Design

- **Righteous** damage doubles against creatures with **Imateria-Tainted**; **Vile** damage doubles against creatures with **Creation-Bound**. See `00_Epic_System_Rules.md` for the precise rule.
- A being may serve a Principle, or wield Imateria-derived magic, without its **morality** determining its vulnerability. Actual Taint depends on the creature's metaphysical nature or transformation.
- A demon or a demonic high priest can wield **Righteous** damage; power used and vulnerability are independent.
- Potential Imateria corruption of **Asmodeus, Gruumsh, Tharizdun**, or similarly powerful beings is a setting possibility, **not yet established as canonical for each**. Their precise histories and Taint status remain to be decided.
- Whether Imateria is wholly outside Creation or represents something more primordial is **not yet established**; do not silently resolve this origin question.

## Gruumsh

Gruumsh represents brutal war, conquest, pursuit, physical domination, and the momentum of the horde.

### Mechanical Identity

Gruumsh mechanics commonly emphasize:

- hunting a chosen target;
- forced movement;
- coordinated aggression;
- expendable followers;
- resurrection/reinforcement of the warband;
- formation breaking;
- gaining momentum from successful melee violence.

Gruumsh protects the **army**, not necessarily the individual.

Low-ranking Followers can therefore have relatively low listed HP while being supported by shared Gruumsh's Favor and Gruumsh's Protection.

### Followers of Gruumsh

Only creatures explicitly tagged as Followers participate fully in the warband system. The horde is commonly coordinated at the encounter level by a `Gruumsh Warband` Non-Monster controller object (see `06_Non_Monster_Entities.md`).

Allies may cooperate with Gruumsh without receiving those benefits.

### Chosen of Gruumsh

A Chosen should feel like a battlefield commander or living expression of the Hunt rather than merely a stronger orc.

Divine Miracle manifestations for Gruumsh may include:

- returning dead followers;
- breaking hostile magic;
- triggering a chain of Hunts;
- transforming terrain into a deadly field of weapons.

## Pandemonium

Pandemonium fills a role similar to a god or power of ecstatic violence, destruction, fury, and uncontrolled slaughter.

Its followers and demons are not simply tactical warriors. They should feel as if violence itself escalates around them.

### Mechanical Identity

Common themes:

- violence generating power;
- power being burned through extreme actions;
- aggression that becomes harder to contain;
- destructive movement;
- brutal retaliation;
- continuing to fight after reaching 0 HP;
- temporary states of murderous ecstasy or rampage.

High-tier Pandemonium abilities should be visibly encounter-shaping rather than merely larger melee attacks.

Examples include:

- throwing creatures extreme distances;
- sealing spellcasting;
- forming weapons from blood;
- stunning or staggering enemies through violent shock;
- crashing through terrain or structures;
- reacting immediately to attackers.

### Relationship with Gruumsh

Pandemonium forces may temporarily cooperate with Gruumsh's warbands, but they are not automatically Followers of Gruumsh and should not inherit Gruumsh's faction mechanics without an explicit reason.

## Divine Chosen

A Chosen is a mortal, outsider, or champion directly empowered by a god.

Chosen are suitable candidates for:

- Divine Miracle;
- deity-specific resources;
- powerful transformations;
- direct divine intervention;
- battlefield-wide manifestations.

The god's intervention should express the deity's identity rather than merely restoring HP.

---

## Epic and Deific Effects

Some effects are designated **Epic** or **deific**.

- Epic effects are stronger than normal magical effects and may require additional defensive resources to resist or suppress.
- Deific effects represent direct divine intervention and may ignore ordinary dispelling or countering unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Use these labels intentionally. They should communicate real mechanical hierarchy, not merely flavor.

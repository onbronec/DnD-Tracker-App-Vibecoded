# Gruumsh Warband

**Entity Type:** Non-Monster
**Fixed Initiative:** 20
**Has HP/AC:** no
**Encounter Points:** 0.1
**Expected DPR:** 0
**Type:** encounter object

## Protective Traits

**Gruumsh's Protection.** This feature costs 1 power point to use. When two or more Followers of Gruumsh are affected by the same spell, ability, or other effect, the Warband may invoke Gruumsh's Protection. Against a non-Epic effect, all affected followers automatically succeed on any saving throw caused by the effect, prevent all damage from it, and ignore any conditions or other effects it would impose. Against an Epic effect, all affected followers instead automatically succeed on their saving throws and take half damage; any effect that normally occurs on a successful save still occurs.

## Regular Traits

**Warband.** Add this object once to an encounter containing at least two conscious Followers of Gruumsh. All such creatures use the same Gruumsh's Favor pool. The Warband stops functioning while fewer than two conscious Followers remain.

**Strength in Numbers.** At the beginning of combat, the Warband gains 1 point of Gruumsh's Favor for every four Followers of Gruumsh participating in the encounter, rounded up. Its maximum Gruumsh's Favor equals this starting amount + 2.

**Hunt Passes With Blood.** A Hunter that successfully Draws Blood cannot be selected as the Hunter during the next Hunt. If the Hunter fails to Draw Blood, it may be selected again normally.

**Defiance Draws His Eye (1/Round).** When a creature other than the Prey directly prevents the Hunter from reaching, attacking, or damaging the Prey, that creature may choose to become the new Prey.

**Hunt Turn.** On initiative count 10, if a Hunt is active, the Hunter takes a special Hunt Turn. It may move up to its speed and make one melee weapon attack against the Prey.

**Draw Blood.** If the Hunter deals melee weapon damage to the Prey during its Hunt Turn, the Hunt succeeds. The Warband gains 1 point of Gruumsh's Favor and the Hunt ends.

## Actions

**Gruumsh's Gaze (1/Round).** Choose one conscious Follower of Gruumsh as the Hunter and one hostile creature as the Prey. Any previous Hunt ends. Until the Hunt ends, the Hunter knows the direction and distance to the Prey while both are on the same plane, its movement speed is doubled while moving toward the Prey, and it has advantage on melee attack rolls against the Prey.

**War Without End.** This action costs 1 power point to use. Choose up to three dead Followers of Gruumsh worth no more than 2 encounter points in total. Each returns to life with half its maximum Hit Points, stands up, and may immediately move up to half its speed.

**Blood Frenzy.** This action costs 1 power point to use. Choose up to three Followers of Gruumsh. Each may immediately move up to its speed and make one melee weapon attack.

**Unbreakable Horde.** This action costs 1 power point to use. Choose up to four Followers of Gruumsh. Each ends one non-Epic condition, spell, or other effect currently affecting it, then may move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Break Their Line.** This action costs 1 power point to use. Choose up to four Followers of Gruumsh. Each may immediately move up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks, then may Shove, Grapple, or use one attack or ability whose primary purpose is to knock a creature Prone, Grapple it, or forcibly move it.
